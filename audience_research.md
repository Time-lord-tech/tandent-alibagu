# Audience Research: Dental Clinic Patients (Ilagan City, Isabela)

## Pain Points
- **Cost Fear**: Dentistry perceived as a luxury; most patients are out-of-pocket payers
- **Dental Anxiety**: Fear of drills, needles, pain — amplified by past negative experiences
- **"Pain-Only" Mindset**: Patients only visit when pain is unbearable, leading to expensive emergency care
- **Lack of Transparency**: No idea what procedures cost or what to expect before walking in
- **Phone Anxiety**: Many hesitant to call and ask; prefer low-friction digital booking
- **Quality Distrust**: Previous bad fillings/dentures erode confidence in local providers

## Dream Outcomes
- A painless, judgment-free dental experience
- Affordable pricing with clear breakdowns (no surprise bills)
- Confidence restoration — a smile they're proud of
- Feeling like a valued person, not just a patient number
- Quick, convenient booking without needing to call

## What They Need to See to Feel Safe
- **Real photos** of the clinic interior (calming, modern, not clinical/cold)
- **Doctor faces and bios** — approachable, human, qualified
- **Step-by-step first visit guide** — "We just look first, no surprises"
- **Pricing transparency** — at least starting-at ranges per service
- **Testimonials from anxious patients** — "I was scared but they were gentle"
- **Sedation/comfort options** clearly listed
- **Online booking** — no phone call required

## Design Implications
- **Tone**: Warm, calming, spa-like — NOT cold clinical blue/teal
- **Palette**: Earth tones, warm neutrals, soft sage greens, muted golds
- **Typography**: Soft serif for trust + clean sans for readability
- **Hero Message**: Address the fear directly — "Scared of the dentist? So were we."
- **Unforgettable Element**: Interactive "What to Expect" timeline for first-time patients
